Quest 1 · Everyday Life

The Fridge Raid Chef

Use when you're staring at random ingredients and have no plan.

I have these ingredients: [list what's in your fridge/pantry]. Suggest 3 dinners I can make in under [30] minutes using mostly what I have. I'm cooking for [number] people and we [any dietary needs, e.g. no dairy]. For each, give a short ingredient list, simple steps, and what I'd need to buy, if anything.
Quest 4 · Build an App or Website

Idea to Plan

Use when you have an app or website idea and don't know where to start.

I want to build [describe your idea] for [who it's for]. I have zero coding experience. Turn this into a simple plan: the core features for a first version (keep it tiny), what the main screens would look like, and the easiest no-code or AI tools to build it with. Explain everything in plain English.
Quest 6 · Gaming

Build Theorycrafter

Use when you want a strong character build without reading 40 forum threads.

I'm playing [game] as a [class/character] at [level or progress point]. My playstyle is [e.g. aggressive melee / stealthy / support]. Suggest a build: key stats, skills, gear, and the order to unlock them. Explain why it works and its biggest weakness. Note if anything may have changed in recent patches.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the 50-prompt pack really free?

Yes. Open or download it directly at /free/50-prompts.pdf, no signup needed. If you prefer email, join the free Prompt Sidequest newsletter and the PDF arrives in your welcome email.

Which AI tools do the prompts work with?

Any modern AI chat: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Copilot and others. The free tiers are enough for nearly everything.

What's in the Bill Fighter Letter Kit?

16 fill-in letters for wrong bills, refunds, rent, credit report errors, insurance appeals and payment plans. Each one has a when-to-use note, the letter with [bracketed] fields, a short phone script and a what-to-save checklist. You also get a printable dispute tracker and call log and AI prompts to tailor any letter. It comes as a 24-page PDF plus an editable Word file.

Is the letter kit legal or financial advice?

No. The letters are general templates. Check your own contract, statement, policy and local laws for deadlines and rights, and talk to a qualified professional about your situation. The kit isn't credit repair and can't remove accurate information or make valid debts go away.

Does the tracker work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It's an .xlsx file with real formulas and no macros, so it opens in Excel, Google Sheets (File → Import) and LibreOffice. Example rows show how it works. Payoff dates and interest are estimates.
