Quest 1 · Everyday Life

The Fridge Raid Chef

Use when you're staring at random ingredients and have no plan.

I have these ingredients: [list what's in your fridge/pantry]. Suggest 3 dinners I can make in under [30] minutes using mostly what I have. I'm cooking for [number] people and we [any dietary needs, e.g. no dairy]. For each, give a short ingredient list, simple steps, and what I'd need to buy, if anything.
Quest 4 · Build an App or Website

Idea to Plan

Use when you have an app or website idea and don't know where to start.

I want to build [describe your idea] for [who it's for]. I have zero coding experience. Turn this into a simple plan: the core features for a first version (keep it tiny), what the main screens would look like, and the easiest no-code or AI tools to build it with. Explain everything in plain English.
Quest 6 · Gaming

Build Theorycrafter

Use when you want a strong character build without reading 40 forum threads.

I'm playing [game] as a [class/character] at [level or progress point]. My playstyle is [e.g. aggressive melee / stealthy / support]. Suggest a build: key stats, skills, gear, and the order to unlock them. Explain why it works and its biggest weakness. Note if anything may have changed in recent patches.
