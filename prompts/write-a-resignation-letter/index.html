ChatGPT prompt to write a resignation letter

Five copy-paste prompts to write a short, kind resignation letter, plan your handover and handle the conversation with your manager.

Quitting a job is stressful even when you are excited about what comes next. A resignation letter does not need to be long or emotional. It needs your last day, a thank you and an offer to help with the handover. AI is good at keeping it short and taking the heat out of it when you are frustrated.

These prompts cover the letter itself, a plan for your notice period, the conversation with your manager, a goodbye note to coworkers and what to say if they make a counteroffer. Leave out confidential project details and other people’s names. Fill those in yourself.

Notice periods and final pay rules depend on your contract and where you live. Check your employment contract or handbook, or ask HR, before you set your last day. AI can help you find the questions to ask, but it cannot read your contract for you unless you paste it, and it can still misread it.

The prompts

Replace every [bracket] with your real details, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok.

Prompt 1

Resignation letter writer

Write a short, professional resignation letter to [manager name or "my manager"]. My role: [job title]. My last day will be [date], which gives [number] weeks of notice. Thank them for [one specific thing you appreciated]. Offer to help with the handover. Under 150 words, warm but brief, no reasons or complaints, with placeholders for my name and the date.

Short is safer. The letter often goes into your HR file.

Prompt 2

Handover plan

I am leaving my job as [job title] on [date]. Here is what I currently own: [paste tasks, projects, recurring reports, logins I manage]. Turn this into a handover plan with one line per item: what it is, who could take it over, what they need to know and a target date to hand it off before I leave. Point out anything I might be forgetting.

Sharing a plan with your manager early makes the notice period much smoother.

Prompt 3

Manager conversation

I am about to tell my manager I am resigning. Our relationship is [good / strained / neutral]. Write a short opener I can say in person or on a call before I send the letter, under 80 words. Then list 3 questions they might ask, such as why I am leaving or where I am going, with a calm, honest answer for each that does not burn bridges.

You do not owe anyone a detailed reason. A simple, polite answer is enough.

Prompt 4

Goodbye message

Write a short goodbye message to my team for my last day as [job title]. Mention [one or two things you enjoyed working on] and thank them. Include a line with my personal email or LinkedIn placeholder if I want to stay in touch. Under 100 words, friendly, no inside jokes I would need to explain.

Send it on your last day, not before you have told your manager.

Prompt 5

Counteroffer check

My employer made a counteroffer after I resigned: [paste the offer]. My new offer is: [paste the key points]. My reasons for leaving were [reasons]. Help me compare the two in a simple table, then list the questions I should ask myself before deciding, including whether the counteroffer fixes the reasons I wanted to leave. Do not tell me what to choose.

A counteroffer can fix pay but not always the reasons you started looking.

Worked example

Situation: you accepted a new job that starts in three weeks and your contract asks for two weeks of notice. You are nervous because your manager is in the middle of a big project.

What you paste: your role, a last day two weeks from Friday and a thank you for the mentoring you got into Prompt 1. The AI returns a five-sentence letter.

Next: Prompt 3 gives you a calm opener for the call, Prompt 2 turns your list of reports and projects into a handover table you bring to that call, and on your last day Prompt 4 writes the goodbye note to the team.

FAQ

How much notice should I give when I resign?

Follow your contract or company handbook first. Where nothing is written down, two weeks is common in the US, but it varies by country and role, so check before you pick a last day.

Should I say why I am leaving in my resignation letter?

Usually no. Keep the letter short and positive and save any feedback for an exit interview, if you want to give it at all.

Can I email my resignation letter?

Often yes, especially for remote jobs, but tell your manager in person or on a call first if you can. Check whether your company expects it through HR or a specific system.
