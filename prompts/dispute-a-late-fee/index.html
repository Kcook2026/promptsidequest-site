ChatGPT prompt to get a late fee waived

Five copy-paste prompts to check a late fee, ask politely for it to be removed on the phone or in writing, and stop the next one before it happens.

A late fee can sting more than the bill itself, especially when the payment was only a day or two late or the mistake was a one-off. Many companies, including credit card issuers, utilities and some landlords, have some room to remove or reduce a fee when a customer asks politely, but whether they will is up to them and to the terms of your account or lease.

An AI chat is useful for the preparation: checking whether the fee matches your agreement, gathering the facts that make your case, writing a calm phone script and drafting a short written request. It cannot tell you whether a company has to waive anything. That comes from your card agreement, your utility's posted policy or your lease, and from the person you speak to.

Before pasting anything, remove your full account number, card number, address and date of birth. The company type, the fee amount, the due date and the date you paid are enough. This page is not legal or financial advice.

The prompts

Replace every [bracket] with your real details, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok.

Prompt 1

Check the fee against your agreement

I was charged a late fee of [amount] by [credit card issuer, utility, landlord] on my [bill or rent] that was due [due date]. I paid [amount] on [date paid] by [method]. Here is the part of my [card agreement, utility policy or lease] about late fees, copied as text: [paste the section]. Explain in plain English what it says about when a fee applies, how much it can be and any grace period. Then tell me whether the fee I was charged seems to match that text, and list anything that is unclear and worth asking about. Only use what is in the text I pasted.

Your card agreement is usually on the issuer's website or app; utility fee rules are often on the bill or the provider's site; rent fees are in your lease.

Prompt 2

Gather the facts that help your case

Help me make a short, honest list of facts to mention when I ask [company] to waive a late fee. My situation: I have been a customer for about [time], I have paid late [number] times in the last year, this payment was [number] days late because [short reason, such as a bank delay, travel, illness or a mix-up with autopay], and I have now paid [in full or the amount]. Turn this into 3 to 5 short bullet points I can say or write, in order of how persuasive they are, without exaggerating anything.

A good payment history and a payment that is already made are often the strongest points, so lead with them if they are true.

Prompt 3

Script the phone call

I am calling [company] to ask them to waive a late fee of [amount] on my account. My key points are: [paste the bullets from Prompt 2]. Write a short, friendly phone script: how to open, one clear sentence asking for the fee to be removed as a courtesy, and how to mention my payment history. Add three calm replies for when they say no, such as asking whether they can waive part of it, whether a supervisor can review it, or whether they can note the account for next time. Remind me to write down the date, the name of the person and what they agreed.

Ask them to confirm how the credit will show on your next statement or bill, then check that it does.

Prompt 4

Write a written request

Write a short, polite message to [company] asking them to waive a late fee of [amount] charged on [date] for the payment due [due date]. Mention that I paid on [date paid] and these points: [bullets from Prompt 2]. Ask them to remove the fee as a one-time courtesy and to confirm the outcome in writing. Keep it under 150 words, suitable for the company's secure message centre, contact form or an email to my landlord, and do not include my full account number.

Send it through the company's official channel, such as the app or website message centre, and keep a copy.

Prompt 5

Stop the next late fee

Help me avoid another late fee on my [bill or rent]. The due date is [day of month], I get paid on [pay dates], and I usually pay by [method]. Suggest a simple plan, such as setting up autopay for at least the minimum, asking whether the company can move my due date, adding calendar reminders a few days before, or keeping a small buffer in my account. Make it a short checklist I can finish today and tell me which options I need to ask the company about.

Some companies let you change your due date; it only takes a quick question to find out whether yours does.

Worked example

Situation: someone with a credit card they have paid on time for two years misses a due date by three days during a busy week and sees a late fee on the statement.

What they do: Prompt 1 helps them read the late-fee section of their card agreement. Prompt 2 turns their two years of on-time payments and the fact that they paid in full a few days later into three short points.

Next: they call using the script from Prompt 3, ask for the fee to be removed as a courtesy, note the agent's name and the date, and set up autopay with Prompt 5. This is an illustrative example, not a promise of any outcome.

FAQ

Will a company really waive a late fee if I ask?

Sometimes. Many companies have some discretion, especially for customers with a good payment history, but none is obliged to say yes just because you ask. Asking politely, once, with the payment already made, usually gives you the best chance.

Should I call or write?

A phone call is often quicker and lets you ask follow-up questions. A written message gives you a record. Many people call first and then send a short message confirming what was agreed.

Is it safe to paste my statement into an AI chat?

Paste only the lines you need, and remove your full account number, card number, address and date of birth first. The fee amount, due date and payment date are enough for these prompts.
