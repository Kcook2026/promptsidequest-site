ChatGPT prompt to dispute a medical bill

Five copy-paste prompts to decode an itemized bill, compare it with your insurance statement, write a clear dispute letter and ask for a lower balance or a payment plan.

A medical bill can arrive weeks after a visit, full of codes and line items that are hard to match to what actually happened. Mistakes are not unusual: duplicate charges, services you never received, or a claim that was billed before insurance finished processing it. Many people simply pay because the bill looks official and calling feels stressful.

An AI chat cannot see your records or know your insurance plan, but it is very good at turning a confusing bill into plain language, building a list of questions, and writing a polite, specific letter. That alone makes the phone call shorter and the letter harder to ignore.

Before you start, ask the provider for an itemized bill and get the matching Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your insurer if you have one. Remove your name, date of birth, member ID and account numbers before pasting anything into an AI tool. The AI explains and drafts; you check every fact against your own paperwork, and for anything legal or about your coverage, the insurer, the provider or a patient advocate has the final word.

The prompts

Replace every [bracket] with your real details, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok.

Prompt 1

Decode the itemized bill

Here is my itemized medical bill with personal details removed: [paste line items, codes, dates and amounts]. For each line, explain in plain language what the service probably is, and flag anything that looks unusual: duplicates, charges on dates I was not there, very high quantities, or items that seem unrelated to [short description of the visit]. Make a short list of questions I should ask the billing office. Do not guess at facts you cannot know; mark those as questions.

Ask for the itemized version first. A summary bill with one total gives you nothing to check.

Prompt 2

Compare bill and insurance statement

Here is my insurance Explanation of Benefits for the same visit: [paste EOB lines, personal details removed]. And here is the provider bill: [paste bill lines]. Line them up and tell me: which charges match, what my insurer says I owe for each, and where the bill asks me for more than the EOB shows as my responsibility. Put it in a simple table with columns: service, billed, insurer paid, EOB says I owe, bill says I owe, difference.

If the bill is higher than what the EOB says you owe, that difference is the first thing to ask about.

Prompt 3

The dispute letter

Write a short, polite dispute letter to the billing office of [provider name] about account [account number, add it yourself later]. Date of service: [date]. I am disputing these charges: [list each charge and the reason, e.g. billed twice, not received, does not match my EOB]. Ask them to review and correct the bill, to send an updated itemized statement, and to pause collection activity while the review is open. Under 250 words, firm and factual, no threats. End with how to reach me.

Send it in a way that leaves a record, such as the patient portal or certified mail, and keep a copy.

Prompt 4

Calm phone script

I am calling [provider] billing about a charge I believe is wrong: [one-sentence summary]. Write me a short phone script: how to open, the two or three key facts to state, what to ask for, and polite replies if they say 'you still owe the balance' or 'that is what insurance decided'. Add a line asking for the rep's name and a reference number. Keep each line short enough to read out loud.

Write down the date, time, name and reference number of every call. It matters if you need to escalate.

Prompt 5

Ask for a discount or payment plan

After review, the [provider] bill of [amount] is correct, but I cannot pay it all at once. My situation: [honest short summary, e.g. income, other bills]. Write a polite request asking whether they offer financial assistance or charity care, a prompt-pay or self-pay discount, or an interest-free payment plan of about [monthly amount] a month. Under 150 words, honest and respectful.

Many hospitals have financial assistance policies. Asking costs nothing, and the worst answer is no.

Worked example

Situation: an example patient gets a $640 bill after an urgent care visit. The insurer's EOB says the patient owes $95.

What you paste: the itemized bill and the EOB, with names and IDs removed, into Prompt 2. The table shows the bill lists one lab test twice and that the provider seems to have billed the patient before the insurance payment was applied.

Next: Prompt 3 drafts a 200-word letter asking the clinic to remove the duplicate test and rebill based on the EOB. Prompt 4 gives a short script for the follow-up call, and the patient writes down the rep's name and a reference number. This is an illustrative example, not a promise of any result.

FAQ

Is it safe to paste a medical bill into ChatGPT?

Remove your name, date of birth, address, member ID, account numbers and anything else that identifies you before pasting. Codes, dates of service and amounts are usually enough for the AI to explain the bill. Check your AI tool's privacy settings too.

Can ChatGPT tell me if a medical charge is wrong?

It can explain what a line item probably means and flag things that look odd, such as duplicates or charges that do not match your EOB. It cannot see your records or your plan, so treat its flags as questions to ask the provider or insurer, not final answers.

What should I ask for before disputing?

Ask the provider for an itemized bill and ask your insurer for the Explanation of Benefits for the same date of service. Comparing the two is often where errors show up. If you are stuck, a patient advocate or your insurer's member services line can help.
