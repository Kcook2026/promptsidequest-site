AI agents for beginners.

What an AI agent is, how it differs from a chatbot, and how to set one up without writing code.

Illustrative demo of the Inbox Bouncer agent from the Agent Template Pack.

Chatbot vs. agent

A chatbot answers the question you just asked. An agent works toward a goal: it follows standing instructions, can use tools or information you connect (your notes, a calendar, a web search), and handles several steps in a row. Think of a chatbot as asking a friend a question, and an agent as giving a helper a recurring job.

The four parts of every agent

  1. Goal: one clear job, e.g. "every morning, summarize what I need to know today."
  2. Instructions: the rules, tone and format, written in plain English.
  3. Inputs and tools: what it can read or use, such as pasted emails, a document or web search.
  4. Check step: where you review the result before anything leaves your hands.

Five beginner agents to try

  • Inbox triage: sorts pasted emails into reply now, later and ignore, and drafts short replies.
  • Daily briefing: turns your calendar, to-dos and a few news topics into a 2-minute morning summary.
  • Meal planner: plans the week's dinners around your budget and builds a grocery list.
  • Job scout: compares job posts against your résumé and flags the best fits.
  • Patch-notes tracker: summarizes game updates and what they change for your build.

Safety rules

  • Start read-only. Let it draft, not send.
  • Never paste passwords, card numbers or bank details.
  • Keep a human approval step before anything is sent, bought or deleted.
  • Double-check facts, dates and numbers.

FAQ

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is an AI assistant you give a goal, instructions and access to some tools or information, so it can complete a multi-step task (like sorting an inbox or writing a daily briefing) instead of just answering one question.

Do I need to code to use an AI agent?

No. Many AI apps let you save custom instructions as a reusable assistant, and no-code automation tools can run steps on a schedule. You write the instructions in plain English.

Are AI agents safe to use?

They are safer when you start small: give read-only access first, keep a human approval step before anything is sent, paid or deleted, and never paste passwords or bank details into a prompt.
